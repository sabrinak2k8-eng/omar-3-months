My love,

Three months. It sounds like such a small amount of time, but somehow you have become such a big part of my world. I wish I could explain just how much happiness you bring into my life.

Even with all the distance between us, you have found your way into my everyday moments. Into my thoughts, my smiles, my little daydreams and all the things I wish I could share with you in person.

I look forward to the day when distance is no longer something we have to think about. When I can see you, hear your voice beside me and make memories with you that don't have to live through a screen.

I hope you know how special you are to me. I love the little things about you, the way you make me smile, and the person you are. I am grateful that our paths crossed.

This is only the beginning, ya rohi. I pray that Allah puts barakah in what we have, keeps our hearts close and guides us towards everything beautiful that is written for us.

Three months down, and Inshallah, so many more to come.

I love you, my Nour Einy.

Forever sending you all my love,
